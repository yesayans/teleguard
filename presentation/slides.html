Our Solution

In-Network Side-Channel Inspection: Zero-App Protection

Deploying inside telecom Session Border Controllers (SBC) with zero audio storage.

1. Zero Latency Stream Tapping

Non-intrusive bifurcated RTP mirroring at the telecom core. The voice call travels uninterrupted while side-channel DSP runs in <25ms.

2. Strict GDPR & Wiretap Privacy

Audio is ingested into ephemeral volatile RAM ring buffers. Extracted into mathematical embeddings; raw audio is immediately overwritten. Zero disk I/O.

3. Universal Reach: Any Device

Protects a $15 Nokia feature phone, vintage landline, or iPhone 16. Protection is provisioned natively at the SIM and carrier routing level.

4. Dual-Action In-Call Alert

In-band gentle audio whisper injected into subscriber's downlink stream + instant Telco Class-0 Flash SMS screen takeover.
